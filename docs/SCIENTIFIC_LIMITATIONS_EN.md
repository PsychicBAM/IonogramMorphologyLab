# Scientific Limitations

Ionogram Morphology Lab is research software, not an automatic physical diagnosis system. Image morphology may be non-unique: propagation conditions, instrumental response, interference, scaling choices, preprocessing and incomplete metadata can yield similar patterns. A candidate label is therefore evidence to review, not a confirmed mechanism.

Parameter estimates depend on the selected method, calibration, trace quality, frequency/time axes, instrument profile and stated units. The application must retain uncertainty, abstention and expert edits; do not force a categorical answer where evidence is inadequate.

Synthetic data test code paths and teaching workflows, not geophysical validity. Internal rule tests or train/test splits do not establish transfer across stations, epochs, operating modes or populations. Model Lab outputs are development artifacts unless independently validated under a preregistered, domain-appropriate protocol.

Users are responsible for provenance, licensed data use, institutional approvals, uncertainty reporting and expert interpretation. See morphology and parameter guides for operational safeguards.
