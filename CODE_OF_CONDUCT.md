# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation respectful and harassment-free regardless of background, identity, experience, discipline, or affiliation. Scientific disagreement is welcome when it is evidence-based and directed at ideas rather than people.

## Expected behavior
Use considerate language; cite evidence and uncertainty; respect confidentiality, data-governance obligations and review boundaries; give constructive feedback; and accept correction when impact differs from intent.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publication sabotage, doxxing, sharing restricted data, or sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement
Report concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports are handled discreetly, with a review proportional to available evidence. Outcomes may include guidance, warning, temporary restriction or removal from project spaces. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
